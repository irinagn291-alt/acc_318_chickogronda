Version 1.0 is the first release of Chickogronda, a one-touch ferry-deck runner.

• Tap to hop. Hold, then release, for a longer leap along mooring plates toward the next horn.
• Cargo crates shorten the jump and fill the "HEEL" bar, up to a full bar. Heel sway moves the marker and changes how forgiving a landing is.
• Four chapters, marked "CH". A chapter card appears at each step, and again at the final horn on chapter 4.
• The live score sits next to "HEEL". The chapter card shows "Score" above "Continue".
• Pause and resume from the top icon or from "Resume" on the pause card titled "Paused".
• "Records" shows "Best score", "Highest chapter", and "Peak cargo". They start at 0 and stay on this device.
• "Settings" includes "Best runs stay on this device.", "Contact", and "Reset". "Reset" clears the saved bests and starts a fresh deck, with no confirmation.
• Opening lines: "Cross to the next horn" and "Tap hops. Hold, then release, for a long gap."

A miss drops between the plates, or a strike on a hazard ends the run the same way. After a brief hold, the deck starts again from the beginning. Quitting the app does not restore an unfinished run.
